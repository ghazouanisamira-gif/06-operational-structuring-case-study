# Performance Management Framework

## Objective

Create a KPI and reporting system that converts operational activity into actionable management information.

## KPI Categories

### Activity

Measures operational execution and workload.

Examples:
- contacts / interactions handled
- follow-ups completed
- activities by channel
- response activity

### Funnel

Measures progression through the customer journey.

Examples:
- qualification rate
- conversion rate
- follow-up completion
- gross funnel conversion

### Operational

Measures reliability and execution.

Examples:
- response time
- process adherence
- SLA / commitment compliance
- execution consistency

### Channel

Measures the contribution and performance of acquisition sources.

Examples:
- leads by source
- source-to-qualified conversion
- source-to-outcome progression

### People

Measures team performance and development.

Examples:
- individual trends
- coaching actions
- action completion
- capability needs

### Management

Measures the health of the operating system.

Examples:
- action closure
- reporting completion
- improvement initiatives
- risk status
- decision follow-up

## KPI Design

Each KPI should define:

**Purpose → Definition → Formula → Data Source → Owner → Frequency → Target / Reference → Action**

## Dashboard Principle

A dashboard should answer:

1. What is happening?
2. Where is the deviation?
3. Why does it matter?
4. What decision is required?
5. Who owns the next action?

## Performance Cycle

**Capture → Consolidate → Measure → Analyze → Review → Challenge → Act → Monitor**

The objective is to move from reporting activity to **actionable performance management**.
