# Performance Management Framework

## KPI Categories

### Activity

Measures operational execution and workload.

Examples:
- Contacts handled
- Follow-ups completed
- Activities by channel

### Funnel

Measures progression through the customer journey.

Examples:
- Qualification rate
- Conversion rate
- Follow-up completion

### Operational

Measures reliability and execution.

Examples:
- Response time
- Process adherence
- SLA / commitment compliance

### Management

Measures the health of the operating system.

Examples:
- Action closure
- Reporting completion
- Improvement initiatives
- Risk status

## KPI Design

Each KPI should define:

**Purpose → Definition → Formula → Data Source → Owner → Frequency → Target → Action**

## Principle

A dashboard should not simply display activity. It should help management answer:

> What is happening, why does it matter, and what should we do next?
