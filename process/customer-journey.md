# Customer Journey & Process Structure

## End-to-End Journey

**Acquisition → Qualification → Service Path → Conversion → Follow-up → Reporting → Management**

## Process View

| Stage | Objective | Typical Output | Management Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acquisition | Generate opportunities | Lead / contact | Source / channel performance |
| Qualification | Assess need and fit | Qualified opportunity | Qualification quality |
| Service Path | Direct customer to the relevant service | Routed opportunity | Handoff quality |
| Conversion | Move opportunity to outcome | Converted customer | Conversion rate |
| Follow-up | Maintain relationship / next action | Follow-up status | Follow-up effectiveness |
| Reporting | Consolidate activity and results | Performance view | KPI trends |
| Management | Decide and prioritize | Actions / decisions | Performance & improvement |

## Cross-Functional Handoffs

The journey required coordination between:

- Marketing / Communication
- Customer Operations
- Academy / Training
- TELC
- Study Visa
- Professional Placement
- Agencies / Centers
- Management

The objective was to clarify how a customer or lead moves between functions without losing ownership or information.

## Data & Visibility

Each stage can be linked to:

**Input → Activity → Output → Data → KPI → Owner → Handoff**

This makes it possible to identify:

- volume entering the stage
- conversion / progression
- drop-offs
- follow-up status
- ownership gaps
- process deviations
- improvement opportunities

## Improvement Questions

- Are leads captured consistently?
- Is ownership clear at each stage?
- Where do opportunities drop?
- Are handoffs traceable?
- Is the customer routed to the appropriate service?
- Which indicators support decisions?
- Which activities require standardization?
- Where can automation reduce manual effort without weakening control?
