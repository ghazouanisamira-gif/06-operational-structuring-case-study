# Implementation Roadmap

## Transformation Sequence

### Phase 1 — Diagnose

Understand current practices, stakeholders, customer journeys, information gaps, operational pain points and management needs.

### Phase 2 — Organize

Consolidate existing information, clarify priorities and separate the work into manageable workstreams.

### Phase 3 — Structure

Define the operating model, processes, roles, knowledge architecture, documentation and governance mechanisms.

### Phase 4 — Standardize

Establish common ways of working, KPI definitions, reporting routines and management controls.

### Phase 5 — Deploy & Pilot

Introduce the new structure progressively, validate it with operational teams and capture feedback.

### Phase 6 — Measure & Challenge

Use dashboards, reporting and management reviews to identify gaps and performance trends.

### Phase 7 — Improve & Sustain

Implement corrective actions, coach teams, adjust the operating model and establish continuity mechanisms.

## Roadmap Logic

**Diagnose → Organize → Structure → Standardize → Deploy → Measure → Improve → Sustain**

## Cross-Functional Planning

The roadmap coordinates multiple workstreams:

- process design
- knowledge management
- documentation
- KPI / dashboard implementation
- reporting
- team performance
- coaching
- governance
- handover
- continuous improvement

## Success Conditions

- clear ownership
- practical documentation
- reliable data
- structured planning
- regular management reviews
- visible priorities
- feedback from operational teams
- action follow-up
- continuous improvement
