# Team Performance & Coaching

## Objective

Connect performance monitoring with practical support, coaching and improvement.

## Performance Approach

Individual performance was interpreted through trends and operational context rather than isolated numbers.

The management sequence was:

**Observe → Understand → Discuss → Coach → Act → Follow Up → Reassess**

## Coaching Areas

Depending on the observed gap, coaching can address:

- customer / lead qualification
- needs discovery
- communication
- sales approach
- follow-up / relance
- process adherence
- use of operational information
- prioritization
- conversion improvement

## Coaching Inputs

Coaching needs can be identified through:

- KPI trends
- dashboard analysis
- quality observations
- customer journey drop-offs
- reporting
- management reviews
- team feedback

## Performance Development Cycle

**Data → Diagnosis → Coaching → Application → Observation → Feedback → Adjustment**

The objective is to make performance management developmental as well as corrective.

## Confidentiality

No individual employee names, personal performance records or sensitive evaluation data are published.
